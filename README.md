T20 Predictor
Divided the international cricket teams into three tiers based on their current ICC rankings to factor relative strengths.

This model calculates multiple projected scores and probability using different mathematical models as mentioned below-
☆  Linear run-rate projection
☆  Momentum based projection
☆  Chasing Factor tracker
☆  Monte Carlo Simulation (mostly for last few overs of chase)
☆  Cap and Balance Logic to prevent unrealistic projections and capping probabilities. 
The weightage and mathematical factors are calculated based on real world observations from the scores of different  matches of the recently concluded   T20 World Cup 2026.
